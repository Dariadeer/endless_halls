*These stats are supposed to modify the way mana propagates through a spell, enforcing some constraints and providing some trade-offs*
- Resistance 
	  Consumes % of transferred mana between 2 slots, resulting in diminishing effect from prolonged circulation of mana without activation of anything.
- Throughput
	  Determines how much mana can be input per spell tick.
- Frequency
	  Determines how many game ticks is in one spell tick.
